Calculation notes & formulas
  • Basic Pension: (Basic Pay + MSP) × 50% — or average of last 10 months if that gives more benefit. (Source: Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare / pension rules).
  • DA: Dearness Allowance applied on basic pension.
  • Gratuity: Calculated as (15/26) × Last Basic Pay × Qualifying Years (common formula used for gratuity). A statutory ceiling (e.g., ₹20,00,000) may apply as per latest government rule.
  • Commutation: Commuted amount = pension × (commutation%/100). Commutation lump sum = commuted amount × commutation factor. Post‑commutation pension = pension − commuted amount.
